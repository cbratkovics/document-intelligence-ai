Northwind Outfitters expense policy (FIN-2024-02)

Sample data for evaluation. Northwind Outfitters is a fictional company.

Meals
Meals while travelling are reimbursed up to 60 dollars per day. Alcohol is not reimbursable.

Travel
Economy airfare is reimbursed when booked at least 14 days in advance. Rail travel in standard class is reimbursed without an advance booking requirement.

Equipment
The company provides a laptop and one external monitor. Employees may request a stipend of up to 250 dollars per year for a desk chair or accessories. The stipend is not available to contractors.

Submission
Expense claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense date with itemised receipts. Claims older than 30 days are declined unless approved by a director.

Approval
Claims above 500 dollars require director approval before submission.
