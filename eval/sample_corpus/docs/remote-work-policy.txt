Northwind Outfitters remote work policy (HR-2023-11)

Sample data for evaluation. Northwind Outfitters is a fictional company.

Scope
This policy applies to salaried employees. It does not apply to warehouse staff, who must work on site.

Core hours
Remote employees must be reachable between 10:00 and 15:00 in the Chicago time zone on working days. Outside core hours, employees set their own schedule.

Equipment
The company provides a laptop and one external monitor. Employees may request a stipend of up to 250 dollars per year for a desk chair or accessories. The stipend is not available to contractors.

Office days
Teams meet in the Chicago office on the first Tuesday of each month. Attendance on office days is expected unless an employee is on approved leave.

Effective date
This version took effect on 1 November 2023 and replaces the 2021 policy, which allowed a 400 dollar stipend.
