# Northwind Outfitters refund policy (REF-2024-07)

This document is sample data written for evaluating the Document Intelligence RAG System. Northwind Outfitters is a fictional retailer.

## Eligibility

Customers may request a refund within 30 days of the delivery date. Items must be unused and in their original packaging. Refund requests submitted after 30 days are declined unless the item was defective on arrival.

## Processing time

Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 business days. Store credit refunds are applied immediately.

## Exceptions

Gift cards, downloadable map packs, and clearance items marked "final sale" are not refundable. Refunds for defective items do not require the original packaging.

## Contact

Refund questions go to the returns desk, reference code REF-2024-07.
