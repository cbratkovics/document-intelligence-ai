# Northwind Outfitters shipping policy (SHP-2024-03)

Sample data for evaluation. Northwind Outfitters is a fictional retailer.

## Domestic orders

Domestic orders ship within 2 business days of payment. Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 business days. Expedited delivery takes 1 to 2 business days and costs a flat 18 dollars.

## International orders

International orders ship within 4 business days. Customs duties are paid by the customer on delivery. Northwind does not ship to freight-forwarding addresses.

## Lost parcels

A parcel is treated as lost when tracking shows no movement for 10 days. Lost parcels are replaced free of charge; refunds for lost parcels follow the refund policy REF-2024-07.

## Contact

Shipping questions reference code SHP-2024-03.
